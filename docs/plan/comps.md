# Competitive & Research Brief (comps.md)

A **research to-do list**, not a set of conclusions. The goal is to map the
landscape ViziThink ([business-plan.md](./business-plan.md)) competes in and
borrows from — direct competitors, adjacent tools, the framework canon, and the
books/thinkers that give the product credibility. Fill in findings inline as you
research; flag anything that changes positioning.

> **How to use this doc:** work top to bottom, capture a one-line takeaway +
> link per item, and tag each with **[THREAT]** (could eat our lunch),
> **[BORROW]** (steal the idea/UX), or **[CRED]** (cite for authority/SEO).

---

## 1. Key questions to answer

1. **Does anyone already do "AI picks the framework + fills the visual"?** If yes,
   how close, how good, what's their distribution? (This is the existential one.)
2. **Where does the framework SEO traffic actually go today?** Who ranks for
   "eisenhower matrix", "2x2 matrix maker", "pre-mortem template", "decision
   matrix"? How beatable are they?
3. **What's the going price** for decision/productivity prosumer tools, and what
   gets paywalled?
4. **What UX patterns** for "chat → structured artifact" already exist that we
   should copy or avoid?
5. **Which books/frameworks** carry the most brand recognition to anchor content
   and credibility?
6. **Is "decision-making tool" a category users search for**, or do they only
   search framework *names* / specific dilemmas? (Determines SEO strategy.)

---

## 2. Direct competitors — "AI + decision-making" tools

Research each: what it does, AI or not, visual or text, pricing, traction,
distribution, the gap we exploit. Tag [THREAT]/[BORROW]/[CRED].

- [ ] **General chatbots as decision tools** — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini,
      Perplexity. *The real baseline.* What does each produce when asked "help me
      decide X"? Do any render interactive/structured output (canvas, artifacts,
      Claude Artifacts, ChatGPT Canvas)? — **closest fast-follow threat.**
- [ ] **Rationale / decision-AI apps** — search "AI decision maker",
      "AI decision matrix", "decision assistant GPT". Catalog the App Store /
      Play Store / Product Hunt entries.
- [ ] **GPT Store / custom GPTs** for decisions (e.g. "Decision Helper" GPTs) —
      how many, how good, what's missing (no persistence, no visual).
- [ ] **Decision-matrix / weighted-scoring web apps** — older SaaS:
      e.g. 1000minds, TransparentChoice, Definitive Pro, Loomio (group
      decisions), Cloverpop, Ahapreneur. Form-heavy, no conversation? Confirm.
- [ ] **"Should I" / coin-flip / pros-cons consumer apps** — lightweight mobile
      apps; gauge how saturated the casual end is.
- [ ] **Anything launched recently on Product Hunt** under "decision making",
      "productivity", "AI" — scan last 12 months for direct hits and learn from
      their launch (comments, traction, pricing).

## 3. Adjacent / substitute tools (the "instead of us" set)

- [ ] **Visual canvas / whiteboard** — Miro, FigJam, Whimsical, Excalidraw,
      tldraw. Do their AI features auto-fill frameworks? What templates exist for
      SWOT / 2×2 / decision matrix? [BORROW] their template UX, [THREAT] if they
      add chat-to-fill.
- [ ] **Mind-mapping** — Xmind, MindMeister, Miro mindmaps, Heptabase. Relevant
      to the canvas/expand-ideas vision in [overview.md](./overview.md).
- [ ] **Note / PKM tools** — Notion (AI), Obsidian, Tana, Reflect, Mem. Do they
      offer decision templates? Notion AI is a notable substitute.
- [ ] **Prioritisation / PM tools** — productboard, RICE calculators, Eisenhower
      apps (e.g. "Focus Matrix", "Priority Matrix"), Todoist's matrix. The PM
      segment's current habits.
- [ ] **Spreadsheets** — the real incumbent for weighted scoring. What templates
      do people share for decision matrices?

## 4. Framework / SEO landscape

For each framework, capture: who ranks page 1, static-PDF vs interactive,
estimated monthly search volume, and difficulty. This directly seeds
marketing-plan §6 Track A.

- [ ] Eisenhower matrix
- [ ] 2×2 / prioritization matrix maker
- [ ] Pros & cons (T-chart) maker
- [ ] SWOT analysis (template + maker)
- [ ] Decision matrix / weighted scoring
- [ ] Pre-mortem template
- [ ] Cost–benefit analysis
- [ ] Scenario planning
- [ ] Decision tree maker
- [ ] Expected-value / decision-under-uncertainty
- [ ] Regret minimisation framework
- [ ] "decision making tool" / "how to make a decision" (category-level intent)

Note who the incumbents are (Atlassian, Asana, Miro, Lucidchart, Canva,
ClickUp, productivity blogs) — they rank for framework terms with static
content and are beatable with a *live, AI-filled* widget.

## 5. Books, thinkers & frameworks (credibility + content fuel)

[CRED] sources to cite in content, anchor authority, and mine for new frameworks.

- [ ] **Annie Duke** — *Thinking in Bets*, *How to Decide* (decision quality,
      resulting, premortems).
- [ ] **Daniel Kahneman** — *Thinking, Fast and Slow* (biases, System 1/2).
- [ ] **Chip & Dan Heath** — *Decisive* (WRAP framework, the 4 villains of
      decision-making) — *very* on-theme; strong content series potential.
- [ ] **Gerd Gigerenzer** — *Gut Feelings*, heuristics.
- [ ] **Shane Parrish / Farnam Street** — mental models, *Clear Thinking* — a
      direct audience and tone reference. Study their content engine.
- [ ] **Eisenhower** (the matrix origin), **Stephen Covey** (popularised it).
- [ ] **Jeff Bezos** — one-way vs two-way doors, regret-minimisation framework
      (already in [overview.md](./overview.md)).
- [ ] **Charlie Munger** — latticework of mental models, inversion (→ pre-mortem).
- [ ] **Gary Klein** — the pre-mortem technique's origin.
- [ ] **Eric Ries** — *Lean Startup* (MVP, build-measure-learn) — surfaces in the
      sample user journey.
- [ ] **Roger Martin / Richard Rumelt** — strategy frameworks (good vs bad
      strategy), for the founder/PM audience.
- [ ] **Game theory canon** — referenced in [overview.md](./overview.md)'s
      concept-search example.

## 6. Distribution & launch comparables

Study *how* analogous tools got their first traction — steal the playbook.

- [ ] Recent indie AI/productivity tools that did well on **Product Hunt** — what
      their demo, copy, and comment strategy looked like.
- [ ] Tools that won via **framework-SEO** (e.g. template-maker sites) — how they

- [ ] **"Build in public"** founders in this space on X — who, what worked.
- [ ] Newsletter sponsorship landscape — PM/productivity newsletters, rough rates,
      audience fit (Lenny's, Product Compass, Department of Product, etc.).
