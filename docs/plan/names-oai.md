## My strongest 5

1. **Forkwise** — best balance of memorable, clear, and ownable.
2. **OptionLab** — best if the app is analytical/structured.
3. **Clearcall** — best consumer/mobile name.
4. **ChoiceOps** — best for B2B/team decision workflows.
5. **DecideLoop** — best if the app monitors decisions after the fact.
