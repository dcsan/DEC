> ### ⚠️ Accuracy — read this
> I found a real limitation while verifying: a registered domain whose owner has **removed its nameservers** answers DNS exactly like a free one (`NXDOMAIN`). So the `dig` pass **over-reports availability on the alt-TLDs**. Confirmed false positives this round: `constella.com`, `bibo.com` (registered since the 1990s), `wuzzy.net`, `cogi.me` — all showed `NXDOMAIN` but are taken.
> - **`.com` status below is trustworthy** — "taken" means real DNS delegation, and I whois/RDAP-verified the only two ambiguous `.com`s (both registered).
> - **Alt-TLD "available" lists are a screen, not a guarantee** — treat them as candidates and confirm the one you want at a registrar (or `whois`/RDAP) before relying on it.

**Verified `.com`: none free in this round** — the catchy/classical stems are long gone. (The three open `.com`s remain `decibud` / `decipal` / `stratbud` from round 1.)

A few **whois-verified open** alt-TLDs worth a look: `neebo.xyz`, `bibo.sh`, `bibo.club`, `zuzu.biz`.
